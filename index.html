Cybersecurity Best Practices

  • Use Passphrases: Strings of 4-5 random words (e.g. correct-horse-battery-staple) are easy to remember and exponentially harder to crack.
  • Never Reuse Passwords: A breach on one service gives attackers credentials for all your accounts (credential stuffing).
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Adds an essential second verification barrier even if a password is stolen.
  • Use a Password Manager: Store cryptographically generated, unique passwords securely.
